New Black Wall Street Initiative

One plan, three organizations. Turn the compass to open a company, then move through its folders. Open a folder to see its documents.

    How it trickles down. The Initiative sets the mission and the order of work. Block Wall Street builds ventures and earns revenue. Sun School of Satoshi trains the people. Building 4 Our Future carries the work to the community with grants and donations. Building 4 Our Future keeps its own independent board. It is tied to the other two by written agreements at fair value, never by ownership or control.

    To add a document, upload the PDF to this repository and add one entry to the DOCS list at the top of index.html.
